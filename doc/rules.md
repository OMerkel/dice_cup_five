# Dice Cup Five

## Turn

Each player has five six-sided dice. On a turn, roll all five dice. You may then reroll any number of dice, keeping the others, and may do so a second time. This gives you at most three rolls in total.

After the first or second roll, you may stop rolling early. Before each reroll, choose which dice to reroll; all other dice are kept. After your final roll, choose one unused scoring category and record the result there. You must choose a category even if the result scores zero.

## Scoring Categories

Each category can be used once per game. Unless stated otherwise, a category scores zero when its requirement is not met.

### Upper Section

For each category, add the dice showing the listed number.

| Category | Score |
| --- | --- |
| Ones | Sum of all dice showing 1 |
| Twos | Sum of all dice showing 2 |
| Threes | Sum of all dice showing 3 |
| Fours | Sum of all dice showing 4 |
| Fives | Sum of all dice showing 5 |
| Sixes | Sum of all dice showing 6 |

If the total of the six upper-section categories is 63 or more, score a 35-point bonus.

### Lower Section

| Category | Requirement and score |
| --- | --- |
| Three of a Kind | At least three dice show the same number; score the sum of all five dice. |
| Four of a Kind | At least four dice show the same number; score the sum of all five dice. |
| Full House | Three dice show one number and the other two show another; score 25 points. |
| Small Straight | At least four consecutive numbers appear; score 30 points. The possible sequences are 1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5, and 3-4-5-6. |
| Large Straight | Five consecutive numbers appear; score 40 points. The possible sequences are 1-2-3-4-5 and 2-3-4-5-6. |
| Five of a Kind | All five dice show the same number; score 50 points. |
| Chance | Score the sum of all five dice, with no additional requirement. |

If you have already scored 50 points in Five of a Kind, each later roll of five matching dice earns a 100-point bonus. You must still score that turn in one unused category as usual.

## End of Game

A game lasts 13 turns per player, one for each scoring category. Add the upper-section bonus, if earned, to the scores in both sections. The player with the highest total score wins.
