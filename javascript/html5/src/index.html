About Dice Cup Five

About Dice Cup Five

Five dice. Three rolls. Thirteen chances to make them count.

Dice Cup Five is a strategic dice game where players aim to achieve the highest score over thirteen turns. The game combines elements of chance and skill, challenging players to make the best decisions with each roll.
